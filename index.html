About the Estate
1987
The Estate Founded
Lín Jiànmíng plants the first Da Hong Pao cultivars on inherited terraced land at 850 m elevation. All work done by hand.
2001
First Export License
After 14 years of domestic trade, Yi Ye ships its first wholesale consignment to a Japanese importer — beginning its international presence.
2009
Second Generation Takes Over
Lín Wěi introduces traceable lot documentation and expands the cultivar range to include Shui Xian and Rou Gui varietals.
2026
Direct Global Distribution
Yi Ye opens direct B2B wholesale to importers in Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia — no intermediaries, full traceability.

Yi Ye Estate is a family-owned single-origin tea farm nestled in the Wuyi Mountains of Fujian Province. For three generations, the Lín family has cultivated, processed, and exported teas that carry the character of the land — not the demands of the market.

Production & quality
製茶工藝
How we process every harvest

All processing happens on-site, by the same team that picks the leaf. No third-party facilities, no anonymous factory runs.

採
Hand-picking

Before 9 am only. Two leaves and a bud — no machine harvesting on our estate.

萎
Withering

Spread on bamboo trays under natural airflow for 6–10 hours. No heated withering tunnels.

揉
Rolling & oxidation

Degree varies by product. Rock oolongs undergo three charcoal roasting passes.

焙
Drying & sorting

Hand-sorted at final stage. Every lot is tasted before packing.

裝
Packing & dispatch

Foil-lined kraft, numbered and dated. FOB Xiamen, FCL and LCL.
